# Creative Writing Workflows

Use this class-level skill for prose and idea generation tasks where the output should sound intentional rather than generic.

## Humanize and voice-match prose

Absorbs `humanizer`. Strip AI tells, preserve meaning, vary rhythm, remove chatbot artifacts, and match any supplied voice sample. Apply this to user-facing prose, docs, PR descriptions, essays, emails, posts, and summaries.

## Generate ideas with constraints

Absorbs `ideation` / `creative-ideation`. Start with the user's domain, constraints, audience, budget, and taste. Generate divergent options first, then cluster and rank them. Prefer specific concepts with a hook, feasibility notes, and next experiments over generic brainstorm lists.

## Final pass

For any writing or idea output, do a quick anti-slop pass: remove inflated significance, stock transitions, rule-of-three padding, forced positivity, and default-safe concepts. Keep the best specifics and cut filler.
