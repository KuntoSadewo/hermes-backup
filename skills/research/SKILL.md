# Research Knowledge Workflows

Use this umbrella when the user asks to find, monitor, organize, or write research. Keep citations/source URLs attached to claims and distinguish retrieval from synthesis.

## Paper Discovery

Absorbs `arxiv`: search arXiv by keyword, author, category, or ID, and preserve paper ids/versions.

## Feed Monitoring

Absorbs `blogwatcher`: monitor blogs and RSS/Atom feeds, deduplicate items, and summarize changes.

## Knowledge Bases

Absorbs `llm-wiki`: build or query interlinked markdown knowledge bases for LLM topics or research corpora.

## Academic Writing

Absorbs `research-paper-writing`: plan, draft, revise, and package ML papers for conference-style submission.
